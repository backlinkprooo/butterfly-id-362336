# Butterfly ID

Free iPhone app that identifies butterflies from a photo, even offline, with a field guide, nearby species and a personal collection of your sightings.

## About

Butterfly ID is a free iPhone app for identifying butterflies in Denmark, Norway, Sweden and Germany. Take a photo or pick one from your library, and an AI model that runs on the phone suggests the species, shows its confidence and lists likely alternatives. Location and season are used as context, so suggestions fit where and when you found the butterfly.
